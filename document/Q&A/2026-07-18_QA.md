# Development Questions & Answers

## Q1

Why is Wallet implemented before Payment?

### Answer

Wallet provides account balance management.

Payment depends on Wallet.

---

## Q2

Why build Transaction before Payment?

### Answer

Deposit and Withdraw already modify balance.

These operations should create immutable transaction records.

Payment can later reuse the same transaction infrastructure.

---

## Q3

Why use BigDecimal?

### Answer

Financial calculations require exact precision.

Floating-point arithmetic can introduce rounding errors.

---

## Q4

Why create a wallet during registration?

### Answer

Every authenticated user should immediately have a usable account.

This simplifies later business flows.

---

## Q5

Why is WalletProvisioningService called instead of WalletRepository?

### Answer

To preserve module boundaries.

Authentication should not know Wallet persistence details.

---

## Q6

Why Flyway instead of automatic schema generation?

### Answer

Flyway provides versioned, reproducible database migrations suitable for production environments.

---

## Q7

Why Modular Monolith first?

### Answer

Faster development.

Simpler debugging.

Lower operational complexity.

Future migration to microservices remains possible.
