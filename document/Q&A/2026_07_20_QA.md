# Development Q&A

Date

20-Jul-2026

---

## Why start with a Modular Monolith?

To build stable business logic before introducing distributed system complexity.

---

## Why not Microservices immediately?

Microservices solve deployment and scalability problems.

They should not be introduced before stable business boundaries exist.

---

## Why maintain an immutable transaction ledger?

Financial history must never be modified.

Every balance movement must remain traceable.

---

## Why separate Payments and Transactions?

Payments represent business operations.

Transactions represent accounting records.

One payment generates ledger entries.

---

## Why introduce NotificationRecorder?

To decouple business modules from notification persistence.

---

## Why introduce Admin as a separate module?

Administration orchestrates existing modules without owning business data.

---

## Why doesn't Admin access repositories directly?

Repository ownership belongs to each module.

Cross-module repository access is prohibited.

---

## Why block wallet operations inside WalletService?

Wallet owns wallet status and balance rules.

Business validation belongs to the owning module.

---

## Why are refunds excluded?

Refund processing introduces new financial workflows and business rules.

They belong to Version 2.

---

## Why is PayFlow suitable for microservices later?

Each module already has clear ownership and boundaries.

Extraction into independent services will require minimal refactoring.
